# Discounts and paid prices: requirements to tests

Written from `docs/plans/discounts-and-paid-prices.md` (requirements R1-R13, owner decisions DA-1 to DA-6) with the plan review's amendments and the owner's answers (A1-A9, Q1-Q32); the case ids (TC-NN) are the test author's. Every behaviour has one owning test at the layer that enforces it; rows marked (wiring) only check that the layers are connected.

Pinned: positions are 0-based where stored (`applies_to_position`, the import's `applies_to`, NOTE data, tool output) and messages say "line N" 1-based (Q25). Receipt-level reasons are `multiple`, `no_reference`, `no_item`, `exceeds_item` and `not_a_saving`, recomputed, never stored. `attribute_discounts` repeats its pass to a fixed point (Q31). The `discounts` NOTE and the `prices` NOTE are emitted by `TracingToolGateway` after `save_receipt` and `save_matches`, not by a node. A negative ITEM line on a receipt a person forced through gets no price observation (Q32). Fixture codes are synthetic (`1362394`, `1543210`, `1234567`, ...); `05700001285` is the marker example the plan quotes.
